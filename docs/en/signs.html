Three locations: three different signs

Letters that look the same on a facade, on a roof and in a showroom are built differently.

Mounted on the building wall above the entrance or along the frontage. They are read from the street and the road, so letter size and night lighting matter. Unlike rooftop signs they need no separate frame, and unlike indoor signs they need protection from downpours, sun and salty air.

  • Wall mounting, power run through the facade
  • Waterproof lighting
  • Materials that don't fade in the sun

Stand on the roof on their own steel frame and are visible from far away. Letters are larger here, and the frame is engineered for wind load. The main challenge is to fix the structure without damaging the roof waterproofing: for that we use ballasted support frames or fixing to load-bearing elements with every joint sealed.

Neon signs

Modern neon is flexible LED neon: it doesn't break, barely heats up and runs on low voltage.

  • Types of neon signs

    On a clear acrylic backing, contour-cut to the shape of the text, or in a box. One colour or several, with or without a dimmer.

  • In a window or on a wall

    Indoor neon for bars, cafes, salons and showrooms. In a window it works as a sign for the street, on a wall as a photo spot.

  • For outdoors

    Waterproof build with sealed connections and a power supply rated for outdoor conditions.

Light boxes

The whole face with the image glows. Suits logos, menus and advertising posters.

  • Rectangular

    The classic for facades and interiors. The image can be changed without replacing the box.

  • Shaped and round

    A box in the shape of your logo or a circle stands out more than a standard rectangle.

  • Double-sided, projecting

    Mounted perpendicular to the wall and visible from both directions along the street. Handy for narrow streets and shopping rows.

  • LED screen as a projecting sign

    A screen instead of a static picture: shows promotions, menus and a scrolling ticker.

Billboards: the economy option for large formats

The most affordable way to take over a large area.

  • Steel frame

    The base of the structure. Size to fit your location and the permitted area.

  • Banner and print

    A full-colour image on banner fabric. The banner is easy to swap for a new promotion.

  • Floodlights and lighting

    External floodlights keep the billboard working after sunset.
