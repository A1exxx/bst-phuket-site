Print: banners, films, vinyl

Full-colour print for signs, shop windows, vehicles and interiors. We'll recommend the material for the job and the service life you need.

Get a quote Message on WhatsApp

Print quality

  • UV: high quality

    The ink cures under ultraviolet light right after it is applied. Colours are vivid and fine details are sharp. We print on film and on rigid materials: acrylic, PVC, composite.

  • Standard quality

    Best for banners and large formats viewed from a distance. Better value.

Banner

The main material for billboards, span banners and temporary advertising.

  • Banner weight

    The denser the fabric, the longer it holds up to wind and sun. A light banner is for short promotions, a heavy one for permanent placement.

  • For standard use

    Banner with front lighting or without: billboards, facades, fences.

  • For backlighting

    Light-diffusing banner for light boxes: glows evenly from inside.

Film

Self-adhesive films for shop windows, signs, walls and vehicles.

  • Standard

    For smooth surfaces: glass, plastic, composite panels.

  • For backlighting

    Light-transmitting film for light boxes and lit letters.

  • Perforated, for windows

    The image is visible from the street, and from inside you see the street. The window works as an ad and doesn't darken the room.

  • Vehicle vinyl

    Stretches and follows the curves of the body. For branding cars, bikes and tuk-tuks.

  • Coloured and printable films

    Plain colours are for cutting lettering and logos. White and clear are for full-colour print.

  • Lamination and protective film

    Protects the print from fading, scratches and washing. Extends service life.

No file for print?

Print needs a special file: at the right scale, with the right colours and bleed around the edges. If you don't have one, we'll prepare it from your logo or draw a mock-up from scratch.
