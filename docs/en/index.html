Common questions

How much does a sign cost?

The price depends on size, materials and lighting. We quote three options (economy, mid-range and premium) so you can pick the one that fits your budget.

How long does production take?

It depends on complexity and workshop load. We give you the lead time together with the quote, before work starts.

Do I need a ready design?

No. We make a mock-up from scratch or prepare your files for print and production.

Do you do installation?

Yes. We install signs on facades, on roofs and indoors, and connect the lighting.

Is there a sign tax in Thailand?

Yes, an annual one. The amount depends on the area of the sign and the language of the text. Your local municipality can tell you the exact rate for your address.

Can I rent instead of buy?

Advertising robots: yes. There is standard rental and a 12-month rental after which the robot becomes yours.
